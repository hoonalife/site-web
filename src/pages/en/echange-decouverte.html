Frequently asked questions

Is it really free and with no commitment?

Yes. We do not ask for a bank card, and nothing obliges you to book anything afterwards.

Remotely or on site?

It is up to you. Remotely, the call lasts 20 minutes, by phone or video call. On site, allow 40 minutes, as we take the opportunity to show you around the centre.

What if I already know what I want?

Then book your naturopathy consultation, a care session, a day or your retreat directly. The discovery call is there for those who are unsure.
