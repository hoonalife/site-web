A simple premise

Our body is a living system with a natural ability to regulate itself. It has everything it needs to stay in balance, as soon as it is given the right conditions.

In today's world, this complex and wonderful system is put to the test. Amid the noise of constant, often contradictory information, we have lost sight of the ABCs: how to support our vitality, simply.

The evidence of common sense means going back to the foundations, simplifying what has been made needlessly complicated, and finding the clarity to make everyday choices that support your balance.

“We are often more attentive to the slightest warning light on our car than to our own body. Spotting the first signs of imbalance lets us act before the glass overflows.”

Olivia De Stoop, founder of hoona

Our role is to help you notice these signals, these “whispers of the body”, so that you can then make informed choices that suit you, with full awareness.

The 7 vitality axes

Everything is connected. Our approach is built around seven pillars that work like communicating vessels: acting on one supports your whole vital energy. And when one overflows, the others feel it too.

Nutrition

Food and hydration are our main sources of energy. We offer practical advice, tailored to your needs, for fresh, seasonal and anti-inflammatory eating, without going to extremes. With particular attention to the microbiota and the digestive system, the foundation of our natural defences, and to the ways of preparing and storing food that turn every meal into quality fuel.

Sleep

It is at night that the body repairs, regenerates and clears out, on every level. Understanding how our environment (lighting, screens, rhythms) and certain habits affect the quality of our nights makes it possible to adopt simple, concrete steps to sleep better.

Movement

Moving in a way that suits you keeps the whole body in balance, avoiding both a sedentary life and overtraining. Nordic walking, yoga, the Alexander technique, strength training: our practices fit easily into daily life and adapt to the weather as well as to how you feel that day.

Detox

Reducing exposure to toxins helps the body preserve its balance. We help you identify the most common sources, on your plate, in your bathroom or in your surroundings, and find simple alternatives. Detox practices then support the body's natural elimination pathways.

“The first detox is to stop taking in toxins… provided you know how.”

Olivia De Stoop

Nervous system

The nervous system is a key regulator of the whole body. When all is well, it alternates between stimulating periods and moments of restorative calm. We share concrete tools, easy to bring into your day, to make room again for recovery, which is often too rare in our lives.

Psycho-emotional & relational

Our emotions and relationships have a direct influence on our vitality. Noticing how certain events, conflicts or recurring patterns weigh on our metabolism means we can respond to them more gently, and cultivate fertile, supportive emotional ground.

Meaning

Finding meaning in what we live nourishes fulfilment. We invite you to explore your passions, your talents and what drives you, to give your body a GPS destination worth heading for: a reason to feel fully alive.
