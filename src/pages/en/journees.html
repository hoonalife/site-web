A day, or a half-day, around one need.

The hoona therapists have composed programmes around one specific need. In a few hours, you move from consultations to care and gentle technologies.

The four themes exist as a half-day and as a full day. Care reserved for people who have already had a consultation is included. The day includes its own consultation, except the “Absolute serenity” day, and the half-day is booked after a first consultation or on the team's advice during a discovery call.

Before you book

Do I need a consultation beforehand?

For a day, no, as it includes its own consultation, except the “Absolute serenity” day. For a half-day, we recommend a first consultation, or a discovery call with the team to choose the right theme.

Is the meal included?

Full days include a lunch prepared by our chef, healthy and anti-inflammatory. Half-days take place in the morning or in the afternoon, with a herbal tea corner at your disposal.

Can I give a day as a gift?

Yes, every format exists as a gift voucher, valid for one year.

And to go further?

Five days, four nights, all inclusive: the immersive retreat brings together consultations, care, group sessions, accommodation and an anti-inflammatory table.
