Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be unwell to book a consultation?

No, quite the opposite. The consultation is designed for prevention, so that you can spot small signals before they settle in. As Olivia says, it is very clever to take care of your health when you are feeling well.

Does it replace medical advice?

No. A holistic naturopathy consultation is an approach to health promotion and prevention. It complements your doctor's follow-up and never replaces it.

Who will I see?

You see one of the hoona team's naturopaths, trained in the 7-axis approach. The consultation is held in French, Dutch or English, according to your preference when you book.

And afterwards?

You are free. Some people leave with a few habits to bring into their routine at home, while others continue with a follow-up consultation, a themed day, care or an immersive retreat.
