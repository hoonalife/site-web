Frequently asked questions

Do I need to feel unwell to book an assessment?

Not at all, quite the opposite. The assessment is designed for prevention: spotting small signals before they settle in. As Olivia says, it is very clever to take care of your health when you are feeling well.

Does the assessment replace medical advice?

No. The holistic assessment is a health promotion and prevention approach. It complements your doctor's follow-up, and never replaces it.

What happens after the assessment?

That is up to you. Some people leave with a few habits to bring home, others continue with a follow-up consultation, a half-day experience or an immersive retreat.
