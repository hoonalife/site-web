Getting to hoona

Steps from the forest, minutes from Brussels

By public transportSTIB tram 44 to the end of the line, in front of the Royal Museum for Central Africa: hoona is a 200-metre walk away. De Lijn buses: lines 317, 410, 519, 526, 549, 553, 555, 558 and N2.200 m on foot
By carClose to the Brussels ring road (R0) and the E40. Access is via a road reserved for residents and local traffic: you are welcome to use it. Parking is available nearby.R0 · E40
By planeBrussels Airport is 13 km from the centre.13 km
