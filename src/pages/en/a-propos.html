Why hoona

At hoona, every care session, consultation or retreat is a chance to become aware again of our ability to act.

hoona's mission is to help you discover the habits and practices that nourish (or not…) our vitality day to day, so that each of us can reconnect with our inner flame, draw on our natural abilities and make conscious choices for a joyful, balanced, independent and aligned life.

“Learning to decode the small signs of imbalance is a major asset for acting intelligently, preventively and appropriately. In that sense, the first detox is to stop taking in toxins, sometimes, and even most of the time, without knowing it.”

Olivia De Stoop, founder of hoona

Here, now.

Where hoona began

hoona comes from the Arabic huna, which means “here” or “in this very place”. The name is a reminder that the resources we need are first found within us, here and now.

A nurse by training, also trained in naturopathy, coaching and integrative health, Olivia De Stoop has been driven for more than thirty years by a passion for holistic health. She imagined hoona as a place of deep renewal, a space for those who feel the need to pause, to refocus and to support their vitality for the long term.

Her personal and professional path has taught her one essential thing: we carry real treasures within us, and we can draw on our energy for our well-being, as long as we are given the right conditions and guided by the right information.

Olivia is deeply convinced that the key to lasting vitality lies in prevention, which means the art of acting on the body's first “whispers”, those faint signals we learn to notice before the glass overflows.

hoona embodies this vision. It is a place where plain good sense, ancestral knowledge and contemporary technologies meet to support you and give you the means to sustain your balance.

The impact of our daily choices on our vitality is huge. What matters is knowing what to do, and how to do it.

“There is nothing more intelligent than looking after your health when you are well.”

Olivia De Stoop, founder of hoona

The team

At hoona, a dedicated team of passionate experts supports you, united around a single goal: to support your path by fostering your independence and well-being. Naturopaths, nurses, therapists, life coaches and other specialists work together, each bringing their interpersonal skills and expertise to an overall and coherent approach.

Trained in-house and driven by a shared vision, our team members combine their skills to offer you tailored support. This diversity makes the support holistic and fluid, able to adapt to your pace and to the specifics of your path.

An ethical charter at the heart of our practice

To ensure rigour and consistency, hoona relies on a code of ethics that every team member signs up to. Founded on integrity, transparency and kindness, it sets out the principles that guide our practice every day and gives everyone a clear, ethical and reassuring framework.

The holistic centre

The place itself is our first form of care.

The hoona centre covers nearly 2,000 m², designed as a refuge. Much more than a place, it is the cornerstone of our team.

Open to nature, it blends spaces for care and for rest in an atmosphere that encourages calm and balance. Every detail, from the materials to the layout of the spaces, has been thought through by Olivia and her team to welcome you just as you are.

Here, cutting-edge technology joins the calm of nature to create the conditions for deep regeneration. It is a pause outside of time, dedicated to that essential moment when we take stock, learn and put ourselves first again.

The Dome

Bathed in light, the Dome is the vibrant heart of our centre. Come and take part in sharing circles, workshops, group sessions or care, such as meditation, gong bath, mindfulness or yoga.

The Workshop space

Our place for learning welcomes you for talks and immersive days, in a bright setting with a restful view.

The Consultation space

This warm, confidential space brings together our rooms for one-to-one consultations.

The Care space

This space gives access to a wide range of care and innovative tools (saunas, baths, manual therapies and dedicated devices) to support the body's natural metabolism, encourage regeneration and accompany detoxification.

Caring beyond our walls

hoona supports associations and people who are committed to others. We offer immersive retreats to entrepreneurs from La Tribu Slow and, with the Teyaku association, to those who look after others. The centre also welcomes teams for tailored team building.

The guesthouse

A place to rest and take it all in

A short walk from the centre, our guesthouse welcomes the guests of our immersive retreats. It is a calm, warm cocoon, designed to settle in, step away from everyday life and extend the benefits of the care and teaching you receive at the centre.

The bedrooms

The house has eight bedrooms over two floors, each with its own private bathroom, with twin beds or a double bed according to your preference. Carefully furnished, they offer a soothing atmosphere that encourages disconnecting and restorative sleep, one of our pillars.

The living spaces

The shared spaces include a lounge, an outdoor lounge, a dining room and a garden. Healthy, tasty meals are prepared by our chef and served at the shared table, indoors or outdoors. This is where the experience continues, over meals or simple moments of sharing.
