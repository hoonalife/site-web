Why hoona

hoona brings together traditional knowledge and modern approaches. Every care session, consultation or retreat is a chance to become aware again of our own ability to act.

Discovering the habits and practices that nourish (or not…) our vitality day to day. Reconnecting with our inner flame, drawing on our natural abilities and making conscious choices for a joyful, balanced, independent and aligned life: that is hoona's mission.

Where hoona began

A nurse by training, also trained in naturopathy, coaching and integrative health, Olivia De Stoop has nurtured a passion for holistic health for more than thirty years. Her path, both personal and professional, has taught her one essential thing: we carry real treasures within us, and we can draw on our energy for our well-being, as long as we are given the right conditions and guided by the right information.

She is convinced that the key to lasting vitality lies in prevention: acting on the body's first “whispers”, before the glass overflows. hoona embodies this vision, a place where plain good sense, ancestral knowledge and contemporary technologies meet.

The impact of our daily choices on our vitality is huge. Provided we know what to do, and how.

The team

Naturopaths, nurses, therapists, life coaches and other specialists: at hoona, you are supported by a dedicated team, trained in-house and driven by a shared vision. This range of skills makes truly tailored support possible, adapting to your pace and your path.

Every team member signs up to a code of ethics founded on integrity, transparency and kindness. It guarantees everyone a clear, ethical and safe setting.

The place itself is our first form of care.

On the edge of the Sonian Forest, minutes from Brussels, the centre was designed as a modern refuge. Bathed in light and open to nature, it combines spaces for care and for rest, where you are welcome just as you are.

The Dome

The vibrant heart of the centre, bathed in light: group sessions, meditation, sound healing, sharing circles.

The Workshop space

Our place for learning, for talks and immersive days, with a restful view.

The Consultation space

Warm, private rooms for one-to-one appointments.

The Care space

Regeneration technologies, saunas, baths and manual therapies, to support the body's natural metabolism.

The guesthouse

A place to rest and take it all in

A short walk from the centre, our guesthouse welcomes immersive retreat guests. A calm, warm cocoon to settle in, step away from everyday life and extend the benefits of your care sessions.

  • 8 rooms over two floors, with double or twin beds
  • A private bathroom in every room
  • A lounge, a convivial dining room and a garden
