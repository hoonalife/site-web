All about the gift voucher

What is a hoona gift voucher?

An invitation to reconnect with yourself. It can be for a specific experience (a yoga session, an introduction to Nordic walking, a care session, a retreat) or for an amount of your choice, to be used on any of our activities, except packs and group session passes.

How do I order one?

Online, through our form, or directly by phone on +32 471 25 09 22 or by email at hello@hoona.life.

How long is it valid?

One year from the date of issue. The expiry date is shown on the voucher, after “Valid until”.

Can I get a paper gift voucher?

Yes: just write to us at hello@hoona.life or give us a call.

Is the voucher personalised?

Yes. Each voucher carries the name of the person receiving it and the name of the person giving it.

Can it be refunded or exchanged?

No, gift vouchers can be neither refunded nor exchanged.

I have received a gift voucher: how do I book?
  1. Choose your care session or experience on hoona.life, then the date and time that suit you.
  2. If your voucher is for an amount, simply add the experience you would like to your booking.
  3. At checkout, enter the voucher code: the amount is deducted automatically.
