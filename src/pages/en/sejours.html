The immersive retreat

In lives where everything keeps speeding up, taking a break has become an essential luxury. hoona invites you to slow down, step aside and put yourself back on the agenda.

For five days, in a cocoon tucked away in Tervuren, you slow down to reconnect with what matters most: you. Here, it is not only about resting. We guide you through a genuine revitalising reset, so you leave with concrete resources and can keep your balance on your own.

Alongside your personal programme, shared moments in a small group round out the support: yoga, Nordic walking in the forest, sound healing and sharing circles. These are simple practices, designed to be carried on at home.

Guided by our team and supported by delicious, anti-inflammatory food, you give your body time to do what it knows how to do: regulate and regenerate.

How it unfolds

Before, during, after

The retreat lasts five days. The support begins three weeks earlier and carries on after you are back home.

Before

We prepare your programme

As soon as you book, you receive a practical guide covering how to get here, what to pack and what awaits you. Two to three weeks before you arrive, a holistic consultation by video call, prepared with a questionnaire, allows your naturopath to build a programme that suits you.

from the moment you book · video consultation

During

Five days just for you

We welcome you on Monday morning at the guesthouse, and you leave on Friday late in the afternoon. In between, you enjoy individual care every day, two consultations, small-group sessions and shared meals. We invite you to switch to flight mode.

Monday 9.15am to Friday 4.30pm

After

You do not leave on your own

You go home with simple habits to keep in daily life. A follow-up consultation takes stock once you are back, the team stays available for your questions, and we check in with you three weeks later.

follow-up consultation · contact after three weeks

Before you book

Who is the immersive retreat for?

The retreat is for anyone who would like to give themselves a deep pause, get to know themselves better, be supported through a period of transition or nurture their physical, emotional and mental vitality. The retreat is open to everyone and requires no prior experience.

Do I need any particular experience?

None at all. The sessions and workshops, from Nordic walking to yoga, adapt to your pace and to what you need at the time.

In which language is the retreat held?

Our retreats are guided in French and in English. Let us know your preferred language when you book.

What should I bring?

We send you a list of essentials after you book. Everything needed for the care and practice sessions is provided. Bring comfortable clothes, something to wear outdoors and whatever does you good, such as a notebook or a book.

Can I choose my room?

Yes. The guesthouse has 8 rooms, with double or twin beds, each with its own private bathroom. When you book, you choose a comfort room, a premium room or, if you come as a pair, a shared room.

Are there any contraindications?

Some health situations are not compatible with what we offer, in particular pregnancy, or ongoing chemotherapy, radiotherapy or immunotherapy. By booking, you confirm that you have read the terms and conditions. If in doubt, give us a call and we will look at it together.

Can I ask questions before committing?

Of course. Our team answers by email and by phone, before, during and after your retreat: hello@hoona.life, +32 471 25 09 22.

What if you put yourself back on the agenda?

A few minutes on the phone are enough to know whether the retreat is the right format for you.
