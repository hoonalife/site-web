The immersive retreat

In lives where everything keeps speeding up, taking a break has become an essential luxury. hoona invites you to slow down, step aside and put yourself back on the agenda.

For five days, in a cocoon tucked away in Tervuren, you slow down to reconnect with what matters most: you. Here, it is not only about resting. We guide you through a genuine revitalising reset, so you leave with concrete resources and can keep your balance on your own.

Alongside your personal programme, shared moments in a small group round out the support: yoga, Nordic walking in the forest, sound healing, sharing circles. Simple practices, designed to be carried on at home.

Guided by our team and supported by delicious, anti-inflammatory food, you give your body time to do what it knows how to do: regulate and regenerate.

Before you book

Who is the immersive retreat for?

For anyone who would like to give themselves a deep pause, get to know themselves better, be supported through a period of transition or nurture their physical, emotional and mental vitality. The retreat is open to everyone and requires no prior experience.

Do I need any particular experience?

None at all. The sessions and workshops, from Nordic walking to yoga, adapt to your pace and to what you need at the time.

In which language is the retreat held?

Our retreats are guided in French and in English. Let us know your preferred language when you book, and we will tell you which dates suit you best.

What should I bring?

We send you a list of essentials after you book. Everything needed for the care and practice sessions is provided. Bring comfortable clothes, something to wear outdoors and whatever does you good: a notebook, a book.

Can I choose my room?

Yes. The guesthouse has 8 rooms, with double or twin beds, each with its own private bathroom. You choose between a comfort and a premium room when you book.

Are there any contraindications?

Some health situations are not compatible with what we offer, in particular pregnancy, or ongoing chemotherapy, radiotherapy or immunotherapy. By booking, you confirm that you have read the terms and conditions. If in doubt, give us a call: we will look at it together.

Can I ask questions before committing?

Of course. Our team answers by email and by phone, before, during and after your retreat: hello@hoona.life, +32 471 25 09 22.

What if you put yourself back on the agenda?

A few minutes on the phone are enough to know whether the retreat is the right format for you.
