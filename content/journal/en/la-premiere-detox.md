The word "detox" is everywhere, often sold in a bottle. At hoona, we start from a slightly less glamorous fact: the body already has everything it needs to eliminate. Liver, kidneys, intestines, skin, lungs, the elimination organs do this work day and night. The question is not how to replace them, but how to stop overloading them, and then how to lend them a hand.

> The first detox is to stop intoxicating yourself.
> -- Olivia De Stoop, founder of hoona

## Sorting things out, in three places

**The plate.** Ultra-processed products, refined sugar, oils heated to high temperatures, alcohol every day: these are the liver's main suppliers of work. More whole foods, in season, simply cooked, and the liver can already breathe.

**The bathroom.** What the skin absorbs also passes through the liver. A shorter ingredient list on the shampoo, the cream and the deodorant is one load less.

**The supplement cupboard.** Piled up for no reason, supplements become an overload themselves. Better a few, well chosen, than many, at random. That is a subject for a consultation, not for a shop shelf.

## The liver, the quiet organ

It filters, transforms, makes the bile that digests fats, stores and distributes energy. When it is congested, you rarely feel it where it sits, under the ribs on the right. You feel it instead in heavy digestion, in tiredness that settles in, in reactive skin, in waking up groggy. So many whispers from the body, better listened to before the vase overflows.

## A gesture at home: the hot-water bottle on the liver

At the centre, the liver poultice combines fresh plant wraps, including yarrow, with a hot-water bottle. The heat stimulates local circulation, supports bile production and relaxes the belly. The plants gently decongest.

At home, the simple version comes down to heat:

- Fill a hot-water bottle and wrap it in a cloth.
- Lie down and place it under the ribs, on the right.
- Leave it for thirty minutes, after a meal or at bedtime.
- Drink a large glass of water afterwards.

It is particularly welcome in winter, when the cold sets in. If a headache appears, remove the bottle. The gesture is not suitable during pregnancy or on irritated skin.

## The bath that helps eliminate

Another ally of the elimination organs: the magnesium salt bath. The heat opens the vessels and makes you sweat, and the magnesium passes through the skin. For a home version, one cup of Epsom salt or magnesium chloride in water at 37 or 38 °C, twenty minutes, then a rinse. Not too hot, and not within the two hours before bed, to give the body time to cool down again.

## And a real cleanse?

It exists, and it takes preparation. A detox that mobilises toxins faster than the elimination organs can follow tires more than it lightens. That is why the holistic detox consultation looks at the ground first: digestion, sleep, stress, hydration. Only then come the care that speeds things up: private sauna, colon hydrotherapy, QRS sessions. The order matters as much as the tools.
